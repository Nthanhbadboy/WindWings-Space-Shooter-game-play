## Review conclusion

The material correction is narrow but decisive: report 122’s three **synthetic virtual-service** sinks (`+10`, `+18`, `+70`) are **SUPERSEDED** by the original virtual methods recovered in 123. The explicit allocator sink and prepared manager/pool/TLS/node boundary remain **CONFIRMED**. Several resulting writes and the source-1609 integration remain supported within the shared published harness lineage, while 122’s instruction totals are superseded by the corrected 123 totals. Raw pins and exact-byte claims are **UNVERIFIABLE** in this review because the assigned review boundary excluded raw/native inputs.
